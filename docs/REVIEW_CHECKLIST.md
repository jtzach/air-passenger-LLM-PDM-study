- [ ] MiD outcomes are nonexclusive ground-mode co-use over overnight air journeys, not identified airport access. Counts describe journeys, not unique people.
- [ ] The three MiD targets are an incomplete, overlapping set; their sum does not define a residual “other” share. Walking/taxi mappings have not been invented.
- [ ] The 23 cards each use one attribute; the 14-attribute screen, two-traveller/two-journey quota, same-data selection/scoring and household-companion definition are clear.
- [ ] Munich outcomes are ten reported airport-access categories from six authorized derived groups. Parking can include a shuttle; expanded totals are not interview Ns. Source and upstream-verification limitations are explicit.
- [ ] The tasks are not treated as interchangeable populations/outcomes, and their MAEs are not compared as a common difficulty scale.

## Prompts and evidence

- [ ] The no-hint arm still contains a different group card for each group. No-group control and constant baseline are separate concepts.

- [ ] Confidential FMG originals, source rows/counts and rates outside the authorized derived scope were neither published nor sent to model services.
- [ ] Model rationales are not presented as observed motives, causal explanations or proof of individual behavior.
