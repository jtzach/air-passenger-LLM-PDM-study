\section{Scope of this supplement}
This document specifies the target construction, weighting, controls, and sequential analysis. It introduces no additional figures or tables; the complete main paper and its appendices are limited to three of each. Exact prompts and all group descriptions are printed in the main paper's prompt appendix. Numerical findings from the fresh experiment must come from its archived complete rounds, not from the earlier exploratory run.

\section{National journey targets and group selection}
The public Mobilit\"at in Tabellen service publishes group tables for MiD 2023 \citep{mit2023}. The benchmark uses the overnight-journey dataset, selecting aircraft as the main mode. The relevant transport question allows multiple answers covering outward and return travel. Car, local bus/rail, and long-distance rail are three separate indicators; treating them as a categorical distribution or normalizing their sum would change the question \citep{midquestionnaire2025}.

The selected indicators are both overlapping and incomplete. The table also records bicycle, scheduled long-distance coach, nonscheduled tour coach, aircraft, ship, motorhome, and other transport. Aircraft is present by the analysis selection, rather than being a predicted additional-mode target. Missing-response categories are not transport modes. Walking and taxi are not individually identifiable as separate rows here, and no assignment of those choices to car or public transport is inferred. Consequently, neither a sum below 100\% nor its complement estimates the share using none of the three selected modes. Recovering that union would require information about joint mode mentions that these separate marginals do not provide.

This module concerns respondents aged at least 14, up to three recently reported journeys with at least one overnight stay, and a three-month recall period. It is distinct from the assigned-day diary and day-journey modules. Individual respondents may contribute multiple journeys. Unweighted reported journeys, rounded survey-weighted bases, and outcome percentages are retained separately \citep{midmethods2025,midhandbook2025}. Weighted bases are not converted into artificial individual records.

The selection protocol listed nine traveler attributes (age, gender category, education, employment, household income, household cars, household size, residential spatial type, and driving-license status) and five journey attributes (purpose, destination region, one-way distance, nights away, and household companions). The protocol was recorded before model evaluation, but earlier age tables had been inspected. It was not an external preregistration or a held-out-data design.

For feature $f$ and substantive category $g$, let $n_{fg}$ be the reported unweighted base and $W_{fg}$ the rounded weighted base. Categories require $n_{fg}\geq100$; meaningful absence categories, such as no car, remain eligible. Missing-response categories are excluded. An eligible feature has at least two retained categories and weighted coverage at least 0.80 of the overall aircraft-journey base. The retained weights are
\begin{equation}
w_{fg}=\frac{W_{fg}}{\sum_{h\in G_f}W_{fh}}.
\end{equation}
For outcome $m$, let $p_{fgm}$ be the observed share and $\bar p_{fm}=\sum_g w_{fg}p_{fgm}$. With binary entropy $H(p)=-p\log_2p-(1-p)\log_2(1-p)$ and $0\log 0=0$, the ranking score is
\begin{equation}
S_f=\frac{1}{3}\sum_{m=1}^{3}
\frac{H(\bar p_{fm})-\sum_gw_{fg}H(p_{fgm})}{H(\bar p_{fm})}.
\end{equation}
A constant outcome contributes zero. This describes variation in the published tables, not a causal effect or a significance test. The two highest-ranked eligible traveler attributes and two highest-ranked eligible journey attributes were retained. The resulting household-car, area, companion, and nights-away cards overlap. Household companions do not enumerate the full travel party; a zero-household-companion category does not necessarily mean traveling alone.

The new experiment keeps this selection fixed. No new group is added because an LLM answers it accurately. Its target remains an observed group percentage rather than an individual choice or an airport-specific access share.

\section{Munich Airport targets and generic cards}
The airport case asks for the reported airport-access category before a passenger's departure from Munich Airport. The eligible population excludes passengers merely transferring between flights. This target differs from a sequence of modes used across an entire return journey and from the departure-day diary. The agreed access categories form a mutually exclusive, complete composition, with a residual category retained where required by the source mapping. They are not literal final-vehicle labels: off-airport parking can include a shuttle leg while remaining in a parking category.

Six generic cards cross business/private purpose with domestic/continental/intercontinental departing-flight type. All other attributes are left unspecified. The classification concerns the departing leg; a connecting itinerary's final destination cannot be inferred from that label alone. The cards do not assert an age, residence, trip duration, airport-origin district, car availability, or joint demographic persona.

The targets are aggregated locally from a coauthor-prepared summary of the airport's 2025 table, using a fixed category mapping and denominators \citep{fmgsummary2026}. The original source workbooks are not independently verified in the present analysis. Unweighted interview counts are unknown, and expanded annual passenger volumes must not be interpreted as interview sample sizes. Flight-region boundaries, including boundary-country treatment, and parking/shuttle coding require checking against the original questionnaire. The prompts' generic operational labels cannot establish exact alignment with that unverified source classification. Same-year source groups are retained; origin-zone distributions from another year are not multiplied with duration margins. Such a product would impose conditional independence and support only synthetic marginal consistency, not observed joint validation. Categorical source segments also do not establish a machine-learning clustering procedure.

Original source counts and row-level extracts stay in local processing. Model requests contain only generic task descriptions and group labels, with no airport-rate hint or observed target values. The six aggregated target distributions and normalized group weights are published with approval as derived study results, enabling offline evaluation. The restricted workbook and its native rows are excluded. A private extraction manifest records source integrity and aggregation checks; the public evaluation configuration contains only the approved six-group derivation.

\section{Prompt conditions and controls}
In the \emph{group/no-hint} condition, the model knows the task, the group card, and the output schema. It receives no numerical overall-rate anchor. The \emph{group/with-hint} condition adds the three public MiD overall rates and is used only for MiD. Both keep specific group answers out of the prompts. The hint is outcome information from the same survey, not an independent external forecast. There is no airport hint arm.

The \emph{no-group model control} removes the card, keeping the task and, where relevant, the MiD hint. It generates an actual model probability vector that is then applied across the evaluation groups. The \emph{constant statistical baseline} instead returns source overall shares without querying a model. Airport baseline values are computed and used locally. These controls answer different questions and must not be merged under a single label such as ``no hint.''

The task requests numerical probabilities, not simulated respondents. Multiplying a probability by 100 permits a plain-language reading as a rate out of 100 similar observations. It does not create a sample of 100 or turn an LLM response into a survey record. An optional rationale is not scored as evidence of motives or as a faithful account of the model's internal process.

Each probability must be a finite numeric value in $[0,1]$. For the airport composition, a raw sum within 0.005 of one is accepted and divided by its sum before scoring; the raw response and normalization factor are retained. A larger sum discrepancy fails validation. This fixed, target-free normalization enforces the simplex used by the loss bound; it does not adjust predictions toward the observed modal shares. MiD's overlapping probabilities are never normalized to a common sum.

The service identifiers are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{google/gemma-3-4b-it};
\item \code{openai/gpt-4.1-mini};
\item \code{qwen/qwen3-235b-a22b-2507};
\item \code{openai/gpt-4.1}.
\end{itemize}
Returned provider/model identifiers, request settings, timestamps, validation, and failures are archived. The four-service panel follows the earlier exploratory experiment; its selection is not claimed to be blind to all previous results. Model-size categories do not isolate a parameter-count effect.

Fresh requests pin Gemma to \code{parasail/bf16}, Qwen to \code{deepinfra/fp8}, and both GPT services to \code{openai}, with fallback routing disabled. Provider/model identity is recorded; a within-block identity change halts the block and withholds its inferential claim. Pinning reduces one source of heterogeneity but does not prove independent generation or immutable model weights.

\section{One complete panel produces one round-level score}
Let $p_i$ be a fixed observed target, $q_{eri}$ a prediction for endpoint $e$ in complete round $r$, and $a_i$ fixed cell weights summing to one. The round-level loss is
\begin{equation}
L_{er}=100\sum_i a_i|q_{eri}-p_i|.
\end{equation}
For MiD, $i=(f,g,m)$ and $a_i=w_{fg}/(FM)$, with four features and three outcomes. Thus, larger categories have more influence within a feature, while features and modes have equal influence. Overlapping categories are not independent observational replications. For the airport case, $a_{gm}=w_g/K$, where $w_g$ is the group's share of expanded passenger volume and $K=10$. The six weights and targets are computed locally and are identical for the model and its source-rate baseline.

Let $b_i$ be the relevant overall-rate baseline for cell $i$ and $B_e=100\sum_i a_i|b_i-p_i|$. Define
\begin{equation}
D_{er}=B_e-L_{er}.
\end{equation}
Positive $D$ means that a complete model panel is more accurate than the constant baseline. A whole round yields one $D$, regardless of the number of group-mode cells. The primary estimand is the median of the round-level $D$ distribution for the fixed service and protocol.

This is different from calculating the MAE of the average probability across repeats. Absolute values are nonlinear, so the MAE of an ensemble of probabilities can be lower than the mean or median single-round MAE. The earlier three-repeat experiment used the ensemble-probability score. It remains exploratory context and is neither pooled into the fresh rounds nor relabeled as a new median result. A single response reproduced to explain an exact prompt is a third object and must carry its own identity.

No-group comparisons use the same targets and weighting but are descriptive secondary diagnostics. Hint-arm comparisons use each model's common complete-round prefix and also remain descriptive. Mode-wise error, signed bias, and selected contrasts can reveal cancellation or subgroup misses. They do not create extra independent observations or additional confirmatory endpoints. MAEs from the two tasks are not interchangeable: one scores three binary co-use indicators, the other ten categories of a composition.

\section{Primary scheduled median inference}
The fixed-look interval uses the binomial order-statistic construction for quantiles \citep[Section 5.3]{lu2020}. The following multiplicity allocation and stopping thresholds are our study-design choices.
There are $E=12$ frozen endpoints: four services multiplied by two MiD conditions plus one airport condition. Looks occur at $n\in\{16,32,64\}$ complete rounds. The total median-family allowance is $\alpha_M=0.04$, giving a two-sided fixed-look allowance
\begin{equation}
\alpha_* = \frac{0.04}{12\times3}
\end{equation}
for each endpoint and scheduled look. The divisor is not reduced if a service fails or an endpoint is uninteresting.

Sort the complete-round improvements as $D_{(1)}\leq\cdots\leq D_{(n)}$. Choose the largest $k\geq1$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
\Pr\{\operatorname{Binomial}(n,1/2)\leq k-1\}\leq\alpha_*/2.
\end{equation}
The noninterpolated order-statistic interval is $[D_{(k)},D_{(n-k+1)}]$. If no $k$ qualifies, use the full prespecified support. Ties are retained; threshold ties are not discarded from the exact directional sign tests. The implementation uses integer binomial sums rather than a normal approximation.

With practical margin $\delta=0.5$ percentage points, the primary decision is meaningful improvement if the lower bound exceeds $\delta$, meaningful degradation if the upper bound is below $-\delta$, and practical equivalence if the full interval lies within $[-\delta,\delta]$. An interval with half-width at most 0.25 points that crosses a decision boundary is precise but inconclusive. Otherwise the endpoint continues to the next scheduled look, or is marked inconclusive at the maximum. Stopping is symmetric: favorable and unfavorable evidence receive the same rule. A global stopping statement requires every frozen endpoint to be accounted for; operational or budget stops must report unresolved endpoints rather than imply equivalence.

The exact coverage argument requires independent, identically distributed complete rounds under the fixed generation protocol. Distinct request seeds are not proof of this property. Hosted services can route, cache, update, or change settings. The analysis therefore records this as an assumption, distinguishes identical outputs from verified independence, and makes no survey-population coverage claim. If the assumptions are not defensible, the empirical intervals can be displayed descriptively but not represented as established repeated-output confidence guarantees.

\section{Secondary bounded-mean confidence sequences}
We use a fixed-mixture betting construction for bounded means, based on the nonnegative-martingale approach described by \citet{waudbysmith2024}. This transparent implementation is not a reproduction of all their optimized procedures.
The secondary quantity is a stable conditional mean of $D$, not its median. Its separate allowance is $\alpha_C=0.01$, split across the same 12 baseline endpoints. A time-uniform confidence sequence already accounts for repeated inspection, so no additional division by the number of looks is applied. The overall allocation reserves 0.04 for the median family and 0.01 for this separate mean family. No-card contrasts do not inherit either guarantee.

The implementation maps a prespecified bounded improvement to $X_r\in[0,1]$. For the nonexclusive MiD indicators, the coordinate-wise loss upper bound, computed from fixed targets rather than observed model runs, is
\begin{equation}
U_e=100\sum_i a_i\max(p_i,1-p_i),\qquad D_{er}\in[B_e-U_e,B_e].
\end{equation}
For the airport task, predictions lie on the $K=10$ category simplex. With fixed group weights $w_g$ and equal category weights, the sharper exact vertex bound is
\begin{equation}
U_e=\frac{100}{K}\sum_g w_g\left[1+\sum_{m=1}^{K}p_{gm}-2\min_m p_{gm}\right].
\end{equation}
When target shares sum exactly to one, the bracket equals $2(1-\min_m p_{gm})$. Retaining the explicit sum accommodates the stored rounded target shares. This bound maximizes the convex absolute-error loss at a simplex vertex; it does not allow mutually incompatible predictions of one for every category. Both bounds were fixed before the fresh model calls. Out-of-support observations must be investigated rather than clipped, and support cannot be tightened using the observed responses.

For candidate normalized mean $m$, each fixed positive fraction $c\in(0,1)$ defines wealth factors $1+c(X_r/m-1)$; the opposite side applies the same construction to $1-X_r$ and $1-m$. A frozen mixture over the implementation's fractions is compared with $2/\alpha$ on each side. The resulting two-sided betting interval is inverted with numerical bounds rounded outward. It requires a constant conditional mean under the relevant filtration and bounded outcomes. It is not selected instead of the median interval merely because it is narrower or more favorable. Secondary mean evidence does not drive the primary stopping decision.

\section{Failures, administrative resumption, and reporting}
The first invalid response or failed request halts the affected block under the frozen failure policy. An incomplete panel supplies no loss; missing probabilities are not filled with baseline values or valid outputs from another round. Replacing failures until a complete panel appears would change the sampling process. Attempted and complete rounds, validation failures, and the actual stopping reason are therefore retained.

An author-requested shutdown interrupted round 7 before the first scheduled inferential look at 16 complete rounds. A separate amendment was frozen before resumption. For every active block interrupted solely by this administrative pause, the entire partial round was replaced using new request identifiers and disjoint seeds. Replacement did not depend on answer values or accuracy. Original partial responses remain archived and excluded from scoring; previously completed rounds and all known failure halts are retained. All original costs, unresolved in-progress cost reservations, and prior active execution time count toward the budgets; only the documented pause is excluded from elapsed execution time. The original plan, raw records, and amendment remain separately auditable.

Fresh-run results must identify the endpoint, completed look, score estimand, margin, uncertainty method, and operational status. Existing exploratory observations can motivate explanations but cannot be inserted into the fresh inference. No claim of individual realism follows from matching aggregate rates. Model narratives are not measured human preferences, and known group labels do not reconstruct their joint distribution.
